Exams

Exam preparation

IELTS, Cambridge B2 First and C1 Advanced, TOEFL. We look at your test date first, then plan the weeks between now and then. We use real past papers and practise with a timer.

If you keep losing marks in the same part of the exam, we work on that part until it gets better.

Work

Business English

Meetings, presentations, interviews and email. We practise with your real documents and your real situations, so you can use it at work the next day.

Perhaps you read English at work with no trouble, but find it hard to speak up in a meeting. This is very common, and we can change it.

Fluency

Everyday conversation

Speaking, listening, phone calls, appointments, small talk, and how to disagree politely. We can talk about anything you like. The point is that you become comfortable.

If you build every sentence in your head before you speak, you will always feel slow. We practise until the words come more easily.

School

Children and teenagers

School work, GCSE years, and children who are new to school in English. I am patient, and every lesson is clear and properly planned.

Some children stop putting their hand up in class. I help them get their confidence back, and I tell parents honestly how it is going.

Questions

The things people ask first.

What is CELTA, and does it matter?

CELTA is the Cambridge Certificate in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages. It is assessed on observed teaching, not on an essay. It matters because it means somebody watched me teach real students and signed it off.

Do online lessons work as well as face to face?

For adults, yes. You get shared documents, corrections you can keep, and no travel. For young children and very quiet speakers, the same room usually wins. Tell me which you are and I will give you a straight answer.

How long will it take?

It depends on where you are now and what you need to be able to do. After the first call I will give you an honest estimate in weeks. If your deadline is not realistic, I will tell you that too.

What happens if I need to cancel?

With 24 hours' notice we move the lesson at no cost. With less, the lesson is charged, because the preparation is already done. Illness and emergencies are treated sensibly.

Can you teach a small group or a team?

Yes. Groups of up to four work well when everyone is at a similar level and wants the same result. Online it is £25 a person, face to face £30.

What if we are not a good fit?

I will say so on the first call and help you find someone better. There is no point in lessons that are not right for you.
