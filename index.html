Who I teach

Four kinds of learner. Four different plans.

Not sure which one you are? Say so on the form. Most people fit into two.

Exams

Exam preparation

IELTS, Cambridge B2 First and C1 Advanced, TOEFL. We start at your test date and plan backwards. We use past papers. We practise against the clock. We work hardest on the parts where you lose marks.

Work

Business English

Meetings, presentations, interviews, and emails that get a reply. We practise with your real documents and your real meetings. You can use it at work the next day.

Fluency

Everyday conversation

Speaking, listening and confidence. Phone calls, appointments, small talk, and how to disagree politely. You will stop planning each sentence before you say it.

Young learners

Children and teenagers

School support, GCSE years, and children starting school in English. Lessons are clear, structured and patient. I tell parents honestly how their child is doing.

Questions

The things people ask first.

What is CELTA, and does it matter?

CELTA is the Cambridge Certificate in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages. It is the main qualification for teaching English to adults. Examiners watched me teach real students and passed me. That is why it matters.

Do online lessons work as well as face to face?

For adults, yes. Often better. We share documents, you keep every correction, and nobody travels. Young children and very shy speakers usually do better in the same room. Tell me who you are and I will give you an honest answer.

How long will it take?

It depends on the distance between your English now and what you need to do with it. After the first call I will give you an estimate in weeks. If your deadline is too soon, I will tell you that as well.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Tell me a day before and we move the lesson free of charge. With less warning I charge for the lesson, because I have already prepared it. I am sensible about illness and emergencies.

Can you teach a small group or a team?

Yes. Groups of up to four work well when everyone is at a similar level and wants the same thing. Online costs £25 a person. Face to face costs £30. Tell me on the form who is in the group.

What if we are not a good fit?

I will tell you on the first call, and I will suggest something better. The wrong course wastes your money and my time.
